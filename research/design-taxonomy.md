# Design Taxonomy

These directions are structural systems, not themes.

## 1. Editorial Evidence

- **Best fit:** Nonprofits, consultancies, associations
- **Poor fit:** Flash-sale retail, very young children’s programs
- **Personality:** Authoritative, thoughtful
- **Color:** Neutral field with one saturated accent
- **Typography:** Expressive serif display plus restrained sans
- **Layout behavior:** Long-form modules, pull quotes, annotated evidence, varied measures
- **Image direction:** Documentary photography and charts
- **Motion:** Subtle section reveals and link feedback
- **Component styling:** Derive edges, dividers, controls, and density from the concept. Do not default to rounded cards.
- **Accessibility risks:** Display typography, low-contrast art direction, nonstandard navigation, or motion may weaken comprehension.
- **Performance risks:** Large media, variable fonts, animation libraries, or video backgrounds.
- **Common AI misuse:** Applying the surface styling while retaining a generic centered hero and three-card section.
- **Codex prompt:** “Develop a Editorial Evidence direction for this verified business brief. Change composition, content emphasis, media behavior, density, component grammar, and mobile behavior. Explain how every choice supports the audience and primary conversion. Do not imitate a reference composition.”
