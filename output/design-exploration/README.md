# Signature Luxe: four homepage concepts

Visual exploration only. Generated using the built-in image generation tool. No website implementation, publishing, or operational changes. Source: repository public content and existing exterior images. Generated interior imagery and new event scenes are illustrative, not verified product photography. All copy is conceptual.

## Brief
Audience: event hosts, wedding planners, corporate organizers, and long-term rental customers in Lansing and Mid-Michigan.
Goal: make the restroom trailer desirable and understandable, with Request Availability as the primary action.
Preserve: Signature Luxe identity, clear service description, actual trailer geometry, public-to-customer workflow boundaries.
Unknowns before implementation: approved photography, exact interior finishes, final copy and font licenses. Owner: site owner.
Repository art-direction reference templates were absent; the skill's required comparison dimensions are covered here.

## 1. Estate Hospitality
Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/46c8dbc5-47c8-4796-b94c-5e46dcda3532
Family: cinematic hospitality. Emotion: occasion, confidence, warmth.
Strategy: sell the guest experience first, then explain amenities.
Type/color: expressive serif and humanist sans; navy, parchment, restrained gold.
Structure: centered brand masthead; photographic opening; editorial introduction; dark amenity section. Sparse density, generous spacing, full-bleed media. Hierarchy comes from image scale and light/dark contrast.
Mechanics: outlined actions and simple links; optional restrained image fades only.
Mobile proposal: compact menu, photo with separate solid text panel if contrast requires it, stacked intro and amenity list.
Access/performance: readable overlay contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive optimized hero.
Fit: 4.5/5. Risk: medium; wedding emphasis can overshadow practical rentals.
Mockup variance: generator used rectangular gold-filled CTAs instead of the requested outlined pills.

## 2. Quiet Editorial
Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/24c0de95-295d-42aa-8240-4e36683cf35b
Family: editorial catalogue. Emotion: composure, care, precision.
Strategy: present the trailer and its amenities like a considered design object.
Type/color: humanist serif with functional sans; parchment, charcoal, umber.
Structure: persistent desktop side masthead/navigation; top editorial headline; rectangular product photograph; interior/amenities spread; ruled event index. Medium density and asymmetrical alignment. Hierarchy comes from typography and thin rules rather than surfaces.
Mechanics: text links and clearly bounded availability action, no decorative motion.
Mobile proposal: sidebar becomes compact top navigation; reading order follows title, CTA, exterior, amenities, use cases.
Access/performance: no tiny inherited catalogue type; readable 16px body target, generous touch targets, lightweight static layout.
Fit: 4/5. Risk: medium; side navigation and understated actions may be less familiar.
Mockup variance: wedding image retained instead of requested daylight exterior.

## 3. Architectural Clarity
Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/934a61aa-50ff-4e90-852b-4ad0b8262d54
Family: modern architectural product showcase. Emotion: clarity, ease, freshness.
Strategy: explain what is rented and how to inquire.
Type/color: geometric sans; warm offwhite, ink, one blue action accent.
Structure: conventional compact header; dominant sunlit product scene; immediate amenity summary; detail section; rental-use grid. Moderate density, left alignment, smaller gaps than hospitality direction.
Mechanics: prominent button and labelled detail links; subtle hover feedback.
Mobile proposal: navigation collapses, hero text sits above product image, amenities and use cases stack.
Access/performance: darken blue as needed for accessible white text, preserve focus, use responsive images and avoid text baked into production imagery.
Fit: 4/5. Risk: low-medium; more approachable but less aligned with existing luxury navy/gold.
Mockup variance: heading is heavier and wraps to three lines; product image partially overlaps text region.

## 4. Signature Estate — recommended
Original synthesis: BelArosa atmosphere + Aesop editorial restraint + Samara product clarity.
Type/color: elegant serif display with legible functional sans; Signature Luxe navy, soft ivory, limited gold.
Structure: slim header; asymmetric ivory text and twilight image; immediate navy amenity ribbon; daylight product explanation; varied editorial event photographs. Alternates emotional and factual content. More balanced density and practical CTA visibility.
Mechanics: primary navy availability action, clear trailer-tour link, no required animation.
Mobile proposal: concise header, text/CTA first then photo, stacked amenity ribbon, product section and event images in reading order.
Access/performance: readable body type, dark action contrast, logical heading hierarchy, keyboard navigation and responsive image sizing. No scroll pinning required.
Fit: 5/5. Risk: low-medium; needs authentic photography and less decorative foliage in final implementation.
Mockup variance: headline wraps to three lines; some foliage was added. Refine both if implemented.
