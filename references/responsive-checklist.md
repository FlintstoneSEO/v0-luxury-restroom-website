# Responsive Checklist

Review 375, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px, plus 320px reflow and text zoom.

- [ ] Reading order remains logical
- [ ] No horizontal page overflow
- [ ] Navigation is task-appropriate
- [ ] H1, H2, display text, oversized statements, major CTA labels, and relevant navigation labels were inspected at every required width
- [ ] No prominent text has isolated final characters, punctuation-only lines, broken word fragments, extremely short final lines, or clearly avoidable one-word orphans
- [ ] Heading measure does not force unnecessary four-plus-line wrapping when horizontal space is available
- [ ] Manual heading breaks are intentional, documented, and effective across supported viewports
- [ ] Body measure remains readable
- [ ] Images preserve focal subject
- [ ] Tables/comparisons have an accessible adaptation
- [ ] Controls remain labelled and reachable
- [ ] Sticky UI does not cover content
- [ ] Tap targets are usable
- [ ] Desktop whitespace does not become mobile dead space
- [ ] Hero and split-layout column starts, heights, visual centers, focal points, and fold positions remain compositionally related
- [ ] Viewport-height sizing, padding, bottom alignment, and offsets do not create artificial dead space or delay primary messaging
- [ ] Repeated stacking does not create an excessively long undifferentiated page
- [ ] Landscape and zoom states tested
